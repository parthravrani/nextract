## The Challenge

A cloud kitchen brand wanted to expand from Mumbai to 8 new cities but had no clue what would sell. Should they launch the same menu everywhere? Which neighborhoods had enough delivery density? In Bangalore, would their North Indian food compete with 500 existing options or find a gap?

## Our Solution

We analyzed Uber Eats restaurant density and cuisine gaps across all 8 target cities. Found that Pune had 12 cloud kitchens per square km in Koregaon Park but zero in Baner (where 80K tech workers lived). Discovered Bangalore had 200+ North Indian options in Indiranagar but only 8 in Electronic City - where orders were 4x higher. Identified that "healthy bowls" had 15-min delivery times in most cities (low competition) vs. 35-min for burgers (saturated). Recommended launching different menus per city based on actual gaps, not assumptions.

## Key Results

- **Successfully expanded to 8 new cities**
- **All locations profitable within 6 months**
- **Analyzed 150,000+ restaurants across 45 markets**
- **Identified optimal price points for each market**
- **$8M in new market revenue**
