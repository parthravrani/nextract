## The Challenge

A US electronics brand wanted to enter India via Flipkart but had no idea how to price products. Should they match US prices? Go cheaper? What were Indian consumers actually paying for similar tech? Launching blind could mean burning $2M in unsold inventory.

## Our Solution

We analyzed 12,000 comparable electronics listings on Flipkart - every smartphone, laptop, and accessory in their category. Discovered Indian consumers paid 15-25% premiums for "international warranty" claims, even when functionally identical to local versions. Found that Flipkart's "Big Billion Days" sale saw 300% order spikes but only 12% of sellers participated effectively. Identified 8 product categories with zero US brand presence but high search volume (like gaming peripherals under ₹2,000).

## Key Results

- **Successful market entry in top 3 categories**
- **Achieved 5% market share in 6 months**
- **Analyzed 100K+ products for positioning**
- **Identified optimal price points for 200+ SKUs**
- **Generated $2M in first-year revenue**
