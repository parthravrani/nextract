## The Challenge

A regional grocery chain partnered with Instacart but was hemorrhaging money. Instacart marked up their $3.99 milk to $5.49, customers blamed the grocery store, and sales tanked 40%. They had no visibility into how Instacart priced their products vs. competitors, or which items were profitable on the platform vs. losers.

## Our Solution

We tracked their 2,400 SKUs across Instacart, comparing against Whole Foods, Safeway, and local competitors. Discovered Instacart was applying flat 38% markup on ALL items - killing sales on price-sensitive staples. Found that organic items had 60% margins even after markup, while conventional produce lost money. Recommended dropping 400 low-margin SKUs from Instacart, focusing on high-margin categories. Result: Same revenue, 35% better margins.
