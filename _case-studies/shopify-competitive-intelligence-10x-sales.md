## The Challenge

A new Shopify fashion store was getting 2,000 visitors/month but only 8 sales. They were pricing "competitively" but had no clue what that actually meant. Were successful Shopify stores using $49 price points or $59? What products were trending? How were top stores structuring their collections?

## Our Solution

We tracked 800 successful fashion Shopify stores for 90 days - every product launch, price change, collection update, and sale event. Discovered that stores hitting $100K+/month used psychological pricing ($47 instead of $50), launched new products every 7-10 days (not monthly), and created urgency with "Only 3 left" inventory displays (even when they had 50). Found the magic number: stores with 80-120 SKUs converted 3x better than those with 300+ (choice paralysis) or under 40 (limited selection).

## Key Results

- **10x sales growth in 12 months**
- **Identified 15 high-demand product categories**
- **Optimized pricing strategy across 200+ SKUs**
- **Analyzed 50,000+ competitive stores**
- **3-month market analysis completed**
