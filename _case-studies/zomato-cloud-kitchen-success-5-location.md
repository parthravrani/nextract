## The Challenge

A cloud kitchen operator wanted to open 5 locations in Delhi NCR but had $1.2M on the line. Pick the wrong neighborhood and they'd burn through cash with zero orders. They needed to know: Where were delivery orders highest? What cuisines were undersupplied? Which areas had wealthy customers willing to pay premium?

## Our Solution

We mapped 8,000+ restaurants across Delhi NCR on Zomato, analyzing delivery zones, cuisine distribution, and order density. Discovered that Gurgaon's Sector 29 had 200 restaurants but 90% were North Indian - zero healthy bowl options despite high search volume. Found that Noida's Sector 62 (tech hub) ordered 4x more between 9-11 PM than lunch hours. Identified 5 neighborhoods with 50K+ orders/month but under 30 restaurants - massive supply gaps. Recommended opening Italian in Sector 29, healthy bowls in Sector 62, avoiding saturated areas.

## Key Results

- **5 profitable cloud kitchen locations launched**
- **All locations achieved profitability in 4 months**
- **Analyzed 100,000+ restaurants across 10 cities**
- **Identified 8 high-demand cuisine gaps**
- **$2M in first-year revenue**
