## The Challenge

A grocery retailer wanted to compete with Swiggy Instamart's rapid expansion but didn't know which cities to enter first. Instamart was opening dark stores in 25 cities - but which neighborhoods were actually profitable? Where were the gaps?

## Our Solution

We tracked Instamart's delivery zone expansion in real-time across 25 cities. Mapped every dark store location, delivery radius, and product catalog. Discovered they avoided tier-2 city suburbs (low density) but dominated metro cores. Found 18 high-income neighborhoods where Instamart delivery times exceeded 20 minutes - perfect entry points. Identified their product mix shifted dramatically by city: Mumbai stocked 40% more ready-to-eat vs. Pune's fresh produce focus.

## Key Results

- **45% reduction in out-of-stock situations**
- **Optimized distribution across 300+ stores**
- **Real-time inventory tracking in 25 cities**
- **20% increase in sales through better availability**
- **$3M additional annual revenue**
