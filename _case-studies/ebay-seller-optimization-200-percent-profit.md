## The Challenge

An eBay seller with 400 vintage electronics listings was making $3K/month while competitors with similar inventory pulled $15K+. Their items weren't bad - they just weren't being found. eBay's search algorithm was a black box, and they had no idea what made top sellers rank first.

## Our Solution

We analyzed 2,500 top-performing vintage electronics sellers on eBay. Discovered that listing titles with 8-10 specific keywords (not generic descriptions) ranked 5x higher. Found that sellers offering "free returns" got 40% more bids even when total cost was identical. Identified that posting auctions on Sunday evenings (not Monday mornings) increased final prices by 18%. Spotted that including "tested and working" with actual test photos boosted conversion 3x vs. just claiming it worked.
