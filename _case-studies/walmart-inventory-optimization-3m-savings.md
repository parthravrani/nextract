## The Challenge

A wholesale supplier to big-box retailers was sitting on $4.2M in excess inventory while simultaneously missing out on hot-selling items. Their buyers made decisions based on last quarter's data, not what was flying off Walmart shelves TODAY. By the time they noticed a trend, it was too late.

## Our Solution

We tracked real-time stock levels for 850 comparable products across Walmart's top 200 stores. When baby formula shortages hit in March, our system flagged it 3 days before mainstream news broke - they ramped up production immediately. Spotted that electric kettles were selling 4x normal rate in Texas during winter storms, prompting emergency shipments that competitors missed entirely.

## Key Results

- **$3M annual cost savings from optimized inventory**
- **40% reduction in stockout situations**
- **25% decrease in excess inventory costs**
- **Real-time tracking across 4,700+ store locations**
- **2-week implementation timeline**
