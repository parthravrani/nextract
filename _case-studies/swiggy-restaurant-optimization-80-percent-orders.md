## The Challenge

A 12-outlet restaurant chain was buried on page 3 of Swiggy search results while inferior competitors ranked top 10. They were spending ₹80K/month on Swiggy ads with minimal returns. Couldn't figure out why their 4.2-star rating wasn't good enough when competitors with 3.9 stars got 3x more orders.

## Our Solution

We reverse-engineered Swiggy's ranking algorithm by analyzing 500+ top-ranked restaurants. Discovered it wasn't just ratings - delivery time estimates mattered more. Restaurants promising 25-min delivery ranked higher than 35-min ones, even with better food. Found that updating menu photos to show "combo meal" deals increased click-through 67%. Identified that adding "bestseller" tags (earned through velocity, not ratings) was the real ranking driver.

## Key Results

- **80% increase in order volumes**
- **Menu optimized based on 50,000+ restaurants**
- **35% improvement in average rating**
- **25% increase in average order value**
- **Achieved top 10 ranking in 3 categories**
