### Sentence Symmetry Analysis

**Pattern Detected:** Most paragraphs are 3-4 lines long with similar sentence structures.

**Example from `_services/e-commerce-scraping.md`:**
```
Extract comprehensive product data, pricing intelligence, reviews, inventory levels, and competitor insights from major e-commerce platforms worldwide.

## What We Extract

- **Product Information**: Titles, descriptions, specifications, images, SKUs, categories
- **Pricing Data**: Current prices, historical pricing, discounts, promotions, currency conversion
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, sentiment analysis, review trends
```

**Fix:** Vary sentence length and structure:
```
We extract product data from Amazon, Walmart, eBay—and 50+ other platforms. But here's what makes us different: [specific differentiator].

Here's what you get:
- Product information. Titles, descriptions, specs, images, SKUs, categories.
- Pricing data. Current prices. Historical pricing. Discounts. Promotions. Currency conversion.
- Reviews & ratings. Customer reviews. Ratings. Sentiment analysis. Review trends.
```
