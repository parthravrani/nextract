## Amazon Dataset

Custom product datasets from Amazon marketplaces worldwide, built to your specification. We collect the data you need and deliver on your schedule — one-time or recurring refresh.

## Dataset Contents

- **Product Information**: Titles, descriptions, specifications, images, ASINs, categories
- **Pricing Data**: Current prices, list prices, discounts, deals, price history
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, Q&A, review trends
- **Seller Information**: Seller names, ratings, shipping options, fulfillment methods
- **Inventory Data**: Stock status, availability, shipping times, delivery options
- **Sales Data**: Best seller rank, sales velocity, trending products

## Export Formats

- **CSV**: Comma-separated values for Excel and spreadsheet applications
- **JSON**: Structured JSON format for developers and APIs
- **Parquet**: Columnar format optimized for data analytics and big data processing
