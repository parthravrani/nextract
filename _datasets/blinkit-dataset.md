# Blinkit Dataset

Comprehensive grocery and quick commerce dataset from Blinkit platform. Monthly refresh ensures you always have the latest data.

## Dataset Contents

- **Product Data**: Product names, descriptions, images, categories, brands
- **Pricing**: Current prices, discounts, promotional prices, unit prices
- **Availability**: Stock status, delivery availability, delivery time estimates
- **Store Data**: Store locations, delivery areas, store hours
- **Categories**: Product categories, subcategories, featured products
- **Promotions**: Active promotions, discounts, deals, offers
