Introduction

Nextract is committed to upholding the highest standards of legal compliance in all aspects of our operations. We understand the importance of adhering to relevant laws and regulations to ensure the protection of data and intellectual property.

Data Privacy Principles

We respect the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We are working towards full compliance and currently implement the following measures:

  • → We maintain transparent data processing practices.
  • → We provide users with the ability to exercise their data rights.
  • → We implement appropriate security measures to protect personal data.

DMCA

Nextract respects intellectual property rights and adheres to the principles of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). We will promptly respond to any valid DMCA takedown notices. Our procedures include:

  • → Designated agent for DMCA notifications.
  • → Prompt removal of infringing material upon receipt of valid notices.
  • → Policy for repeat infringers.

Ethical Web Scraping Practices

We are committed to ethical web scraping practices. This includes:

  • → Respecting robots.txt files.
  • → Adhering to website terms of use.
  • → Ensuring that data collection is conducted in a responsible and legal manner.
