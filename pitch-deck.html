The problem

External data is critical.
Reliable external data is rare.

Most teams we talk to have tried one of three things: a DIY scraping team, a generic API tool, or an offshore agency. All three tend to break in the same ways.

DIY Infrastructure
Engineering time disappears into scraping
Proxy rotation, CAPTCHA, headless browsers, site changes. None of it is your product.
Brittle pipelines
Feeds break the week you need them most
A launch, a promo, a board deck — and the pipeline silently stopped three days ago.
Data quality roulette
You can’t tell if the numbers are right
Missing rows, stale fields, mismatched SKUs. Decisions get made on noisy inputs.
Maintenance tax
The scope never actually shrinks
Every quarter adds a platform, a field, a region — and more infra no one wants to own.
