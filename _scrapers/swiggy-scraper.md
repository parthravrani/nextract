# Swiggy Scraper

Extract comprehensive food delivery data including restaurant menus, prices, reviews, ratings, and delivery information from Swiggy platform.

## What You Can Extract

- **Restaurant Data**: Restaurant names, addresses, cuisines, ratings, delivery times
- **Menu Data**: Dishes, descriptions, prices, categories, dietary information
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, photos, sentiment analysis
- **Delivery Data**: Delivery fees, minimum orders, delivery time estimates, coverage areas
- **Promotions**: Active promotions, discounts, deals, offers
- **Operational Data**: Restaurant hours, availability, peak times

## Use Cases

- **Menu Intelligence**: Track menu changes, price updates, new items
- **Price Monitoring**: Monitor Swiggy pricing, track competitor prices
- **Review Monitoring**: Track reviews, monitor brand reputation
- **Market Research**: Analyze restaurant trends, popular cuisines, pricing strategies
- **Food Delivery Intelligence**: Power your food delivery strategy with Swiggy data

## Features

- **Real-time Updates**: Get menu and price changes as they happen
- **High Success Rate**: High success rate for data extraction
- **Scalable**: Handle thousands of restaurants and menu items
- **Structured Data**: Clean, normalized JSON data ready for your applications
- **Geographic Coverage**: Support for multiple cities and regions
