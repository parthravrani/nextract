# TikTok Scraper

Extract comprehensive TikTok data including videos, user profiles, engagement metrics, trends, hashtags, and audience insights.

## What You Can Extract

- **Videos**: Video URLs, captions, hashtags, music, duration, timestamps
- **User Profiles**: Username, bio, followers, following, likes, video count
- **Engagement Metrics**: Views, likes, shares, comments, engagement rates
- **Trends & Hashtags**: Trending hashtags, hashtag performance, viral content
