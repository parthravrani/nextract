# Zillow Scraper

Extract comprehensive property data including listings, prices, details, images, locations, and market trends from Zillow.

## What You Can Extract

- **Property Listings**: Address, price, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, property type
- **Property Details**: Descriptions, features, amenities, year built, lot size
- **Images & Media**: Property photos, virtual tours, floor plans, neighborhood images
- **Location Data**: Coordinates, neighborhood information, school districts, walkability scores
- **Market Data**: Price history, Zestimate, price per square foot, market trends
- **Agent Information**: Listing agent, broker details, contact information
