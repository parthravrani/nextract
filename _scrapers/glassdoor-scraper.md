# Glassdoor Scraper

Extract comprehensive job and company data including listings, reviews, salaries, interview reviews, and employer information from Glassdoor.

## What You Can Extract

- **Job Listings**: Title, description, requirements, location, salary, company
- **Company Reviews**: Employee reviews, ratings, pros/cons, CEO approval
- **Salary Data**: Salary ranges, compensation packages, benefits, equity
- **Interview Reviews**: Interview experiences, questions, difficulty ratings
- **Company Data**: Company name, size, industry, location, company culture
- **Benefits**: Benefits information, perks, work-life balance ratings

## Use Cases

- **Job Aggregation**: Power job search engines, aggregators, and marketplaces
- **Salary Intelligence**: Track salary trends, benchmark compensation, market analysis
- **Talent Acquisition**: Find candidates, analyze job market, optimize recruitment
- **Employer Branding**: Monitor company reputation, track reviews, improve employer brand
- **Market Research**: Understand hiring trends, skill demand, industry insights

## Features

- **Comprehensive Data**: Extract all relevant job and company data
- **High Success Rate**: Reliable extraction despite Glassdoor's restrictions
- **Real-time Updates**: Get new job postings and reviews as they're published
- **Scalable**: Handle millions of job listings and reviews
