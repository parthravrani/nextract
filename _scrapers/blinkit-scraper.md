# Blinkit Scraper

Extract comprehensive grocery and quick commerce data including products, prices, availability, and delivery information from Blinkit platform.

## What You Can Extract

- **Product Data**: Product names, descriptions, images, categories, brands
- **Pricing**: Current prices, discounts, promotional prices, unit prices
- **Availability**: Stock status, delivery availability, delivery time estimates
- **Store Data**: Store locations, delivery areas, store hours
- **Categories**: Product categories, subcategories, featured products
- **Promotions**: Active promotions, discounts, deals, offers

## Use Cases

- **Price Intelligence**: Monitor Blinkit pricing, track competitor prices
- **Product Research**: Track products, analyze trends, identify opportunities
- **Quick Commerce Intelligence**: Power your quick commerce strategy with Blinkit data
- **Market Analysis**: Understand quick commerce market dynamics
- **Delivery Intelligence**: Analyze delivery times, coverage areas, availability

## Features

- **Real-time Data**: Get product and price updates as they happen
- **High Success Rate**: High success rate for data extraction
- **Scalable**: Handle thousands of products and stores
- **Structured Data**: Clean, normalized JSON data ready for your applications
- **Geographic Coverage**: Support for multiple cities and regions
