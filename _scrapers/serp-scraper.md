# SERP Scraper

Extract comprehensive search engine results data including organic results, paid ads, featured snippets, local packs, and SEO metrics from Google, Bing, and other search engines.

## What You Can Extract

- **Organic Results**: Titles, descriptions, URLs, rankings, snippets
- **Paid Ads**: Ad copy, headlines, URLs, ad positions, extensions
- **Featured Snippets**: Featured snippet content, position, format
- **Local Pack**: Local business results, maps, reviews, contact information
- **People Also Ask**: Related questions, answers, expanded results
- **Image Results**: Image URLs, titles, sources, dimensions
- **Video Results**: Video thumbnails, titles, sources, durations

## Use Cases

- **SEO Monitoring**: Track keyword rankings, monitor SERP changes, analyze competition
- **Market Research**: Understand search trends, analyze competitor strategies
- **Ad Intelligence**: Monitor competitor ads, analyze ad strategies, optimize campaigns
- **Local SEO**: Track local rankings, monitor local pack positions, analyze local competition
- **Content Research**: Identify content opportunities, analyze top-performing content

## Features

- **Multi-Engine Support**: Google, Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, and more
- **Comprehensive Data**: Extract all SERP elements in structured format
- **High Success Rate**: Reliable extraction despite search engine restrictions
- **Real-time Updates**: Get SERP changes as they happen
- **Geographic Targeting**: Support for location-specific searches worldwide

- **Organic Results**: Complete organic search results with rankings
- **Paid Ads**: All paid ad data with positions and extensions
