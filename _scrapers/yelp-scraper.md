# Yelp Scraper

Extract comprehensive business data including listings, reviews, ratings, photos, hours, and location information from Yelp.

## What You Can Extract

- **Business Listings**: Name, address, phone, website, categories, hours
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, review dates, reviewer information
- **Photos**: Business photos, food photos, interior photos, user-submitted images
- **Location Data**: Address, coordinates, neighborhood, map data
- **Business Details**: Amenities, price range, attributes, health scores
- **Check-ins**: Check-in data, popular times, visitor patterns
