# Amazon Scraper

Extract comprehensive product data, pricing, reviews, ratings, seller information, and inventory data from Amazon with our specialized Amazon scraper.

## What You Can Extract

- **Product Data**: Titles, descriptions, images, ASINs, categories, specifications
- **Pricing**: Current prices, list prices, discounts, deals, price history
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, Q&A, review trends
- **Seller Information**: Seller names, ratings, shipping options, fulfillment methods
- **Inventory**: Stock status, availability, shipping times, delivery options
- **Sales Data**: Best seller rank, sales velocity, trending products

- **Price Intelligence**: Monitor competitor pricing, track price changes, optimize your pricing

- **Review Monitoring**: Track product reviews, monitor brand reputation, analyze sentiment

- **E-commerce Intelligence**: Power your e-commerce strategy with real-time Amazon data
