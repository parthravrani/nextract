# Shopify Store Scraper

Extract comprehensive product data, pricing, reviews, inventory, and store information from any Shopify-powered e-commerce store.

## What You Can Extract

- **Product Data**: Titles, descriptions, images, variants, SKUs, categories
- **Pricing**: Current prices, compare-at prices, sale prices, currency conversion
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, Q&A, review trends
- **Inventory**: Stock levels, availability status, variant availability
- **Store Data**: Store information, policies, shipping options
- **Collections**: Product collections, featured products, categories

## Use Cases

- **Price Intelligence**: Monitor competitor pricing across Shopify stores
- **Product Research**: Track products, analyze trends, identify opportunities
- **Market Analysis**: Understand Shopify store market dynamics
- **Brand Monitoring**: Monitor brand presence across Shopify stores
- **E-commerce Intelligence**: Power your e-commerce strategy with Shopify data
