# LinkedIn Scraper

Extract comprehensive professional network data including profiles, job listings, company information, and network insights from LinkedIn.

## What You Can Extract

- **Profiles**: Name, headline, summary, experience, education, skills, connections
- **Job Listings**: Title, description, requirements, company, location, salary
- **Company Data**: Company name, size, industry, employees, company updates
- **Posts & Content**: Posts, articles, engagement metrics, comments, shares
- **Network Data**: Connections, mutual connections, network insights
- **Recruitment Data**: Recruiter information, hiring trends, talent pool data

## Use Cases

- **Lead Generation**: Extract B2B leads, contact information, decision makers
- **Recruitment**: Find candidates, analyze job market, optimize hiring
- **Market Research**: Understand industry trends, analyze competitor strategies
- **Sales Intelligence**: Identify prospects, research companies, track decision makers
- **Brand Monitoring**: Track brand mentions, monitor reputation, analyze engagement
