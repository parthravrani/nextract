What We Check

Listings audited,
field by field

Titles & descriptions

Compared against your golden master. We catch unauthorised edits, truncated titles, and missing keywords.

Images & A+ content

Image count, order, main image spec compliance, A+ modules present or stripped, perceptual image hashing to detect substitutions.

MRP & pricing fields

MRP, sale price, strike-through, and unit price sanity checks. Pricing errors get detected fast.

Attributes & specs

Size, weight, material, country of origin, care instructions — every attribute checked against your source data.

Seller / brand store

Is the listing still attributed to your authorised seller? Or did a reseller seize the canonical PDP?

Compliance flags

Missing mandatory disclosures, expired offers, or policy-violating claims — surfaced automatically.

Output

You get a list of fixes, not a wall of data.

Every audit produces a per-SKU variance report — what's correct, what's drifted, where it drifted, and who can fix it. Feeds into your PIM or ticketing tool directly.

Delivery options
  • Daily or weekly variance report (CSV/JSON)
  • Webhook on every detected change
  • Direct push into PIM (Salsify, Akeneo, custom)
  • Snowflake / BigQuery / S3 loads
  • Slack-based alerting for critical fields
