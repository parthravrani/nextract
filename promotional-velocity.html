What We Track

Not a price. A pattern.

Promo frequency

How many days per month a SKU is on offer. The "always-on" vs "event-driven" split is visible instantly.

Discount depth

Not just "on offer" — how deep (avg, median, max discount) with a full distribution.

Protected SKUs

SKUs that rarely or never discount — the core revenue drivers you want to understand.

Sacrifice SKUs

SKUs used as discount bait to pull traffic. Important to identify before pricing against them.

Offer types

Flat discount, combo, BOGO, free delivery, bank offers, coupon stacking — normalised into one schema.

Seasonality

Festive ramps, salary-cycle discounting, monsoon category dynamics — detected from history, not assumed.
