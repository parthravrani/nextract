Every move.

Indian e-commerce moves fast. Prices change by the hour on Amazon. A competitor goes OOS on Blinkit and your rank shifts overnight. Traditional BI tools can't keep up with that cadence.

Nextract was built to close that gap. We continuously crawl product listings, catalogue pages, pricing, stock signals, seller data, and rankings — then deliver it as clean, structured data through whichever surface your team already uses: a spreadsheet, a REST API call, or a live dashboard.
