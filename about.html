Our Story

Why we started Nextract.

We've built data infrastructure at scale. We've dealt with anti-bot systems, rate limits, and the complexity of maintaining reliable data pipelines.

We started Nextract because we saw businesses spending too much engineering time on data extraction instead of building their core products.

Our mission is simple: handle the complexity of data extraction so businesses can focus on using data, not collecting it.
