# Healthcare Data Scraping Services

Extract comprehensive healthcare data including drug information, medical device data, healthcare provider information, and medical research data from healthcare platforms.

## What We Extract

- **Drug Data**: Drug names, dosages, prices, availability, side effects, interactions
- **Medical Device Data**: Device specifications, prices, availability, regulatory information
- **Provider Data**: Doctor profiles, specialties, locations, ratings, availability
- **Medical Research**: Research papers, clinical trials, medical news, publications
- **Insurance Data**: Coverage information, claim data, provider networks
- **Healthcare Facilities**: Hospital information, services, ratings, locations

## Use Cases

- **Drug Intelligence**: Track drug prices, monitor availability, analyze market trends
- **Provider Research**: Find healthcare providers, analyze ratings, optimize provider networks
- **Medical Research**: Track research publications, monitor clinical trials, analyze trends
- **Healthcare Platforms**: Power health tech apps, telemedicine platforms, provider directories
- **Market Analysis**: Analyze healthcare market trends, pricing strategies, competitive landscape

WebMD, Drugs.com, Healthgrades, Vitals, medical device websites, pharmaceutical websites, and healthcare platforms globally.
