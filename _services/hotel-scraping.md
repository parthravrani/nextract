# Hotel Data Scraping Services

Extract comprehensive hotel data including prices, availability, reviews, amenities, locations, and booking information from major travel and accommodation platforms.

## What We Extract

- **Hotel Information**: Name, address, star rating, amenities, room types, policies
- **Pricing Data**: Room rates, taxes, fees, seasonal pricing, discount offers
- **Availability**: Real-time availability, booking calendars, room inventory
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Guest reviews, ratings, photos, sentiment analysis
- **Location Data**: Coordinates, nearby attractions, transportation options
- **Booking Data**: Booking policies, cancellation terms, payment options

## Use Cases

- **Price Intelligence**: Monitor competitor pricing, optimize your rates, dynamic pricing strategies
- **Market Research**: Analyze hotel trends, occupancy rates, seasonal patterns
- **Competitive Analysis**: Track competitor amenities, pricing strategies, market positioning
- **Review Monitoring**: Track guest sentiment, respond to reviews, monitor brand reputation
- **Travel Platforms**: Power OTAs, metasearch engines, booking engines with real-time data

## Platforms We Support

Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda, Airbnb, TripAdvisor, Google Hotels, Kayak, Trivago, and hotel websites globally.
