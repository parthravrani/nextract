# Finance & Banking Data Scraping Services

Extract comprehensive financial data including stock prices, market data, loan rates, banking information, and economic indicators from financial platforms and institutions.

## What We Extract

- **Stock Data**: Prices, volumes, market cap, P/E ratios, dividends, historical data
- **Market Data**: Indices, commodities, currencies, bonds, derivatives
- **Banking Data**: Interest rates, loan rates, credit card offers, account features
- **Financial News**: News articles, analyst reports, market commentary, sentiment
- **Company Data**: Financial statements, earnings reports, SEC filings, corporate actions
- **Economic Indicators**: GDP, inflation, employment data, central bank rates

## Use Cases

- **Investment Research**: Track stock performance, analyze market trends, portfolio optimization
- **Price Monitoring**: Monitor loan rates, credit card offers, banking products
- **Market Intelligence**: Analyze market movements, identify opportunities, risk assessment
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Track regulatory changes, compliance requirements, reporting
- **Fintech Platforms**: Power trading platforms, investment apps, financial aggregators

## Platforms We Support

Yahoo Finance, Bloomberg, Reuters, Financial Times, SEC EDGAR, banking websites, loan comparison sites, and financial data providers globally.
