# Car Rental Data Scraping Services

Extract comprehensive car rental data including prices, availability, vehicle details, locations, and booking information from major car rental platforms.

## What We Extract

- **Vehicle Data**: Make, model, year, features, fuel type, capacity, images
- **Pricing Data**: Daily rates, weekly rates, monthly rates, fees, insurance costs
- **Availability**: Real-time availability, booking calendars, pickup/dropoff locations
- **Rental Terms**: Rental policies, age requirements, driver requirements, mileage limits
- **Location Data**: Pickup/dropoff locations, office addresses, operating hours
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Customer reviews, ratings, rental experience feedback

## Use Cases

- **Price Intelligence**: Monitor competitor pricing, optimize rates, dynamic pricing strategies
- **Market Research**: Analyze rental trends, seasonal patterns, demand forecasting
- **Aggregation Platforms**: Power car rental aggregators, comparison sites, booking platforms
- **Fleet Management**: Track vehicle availability, optimize fleet utilization, pricing strategies
- **Mobility Tech**: Power mobility apps, car-sharing platforms, rental management systems
