## 2. The Technical Health Check

### Title Tag Analysis

#### Issue: Keyword-Stuffed Titles (Not Human-Optimized)

**Current Pattern:**
```
"Nextract | E-commerce Scraping | Amazon, Walmart, eBay"
"Nextract | Price Intelligence | Competitive Pricing"
"Nextract | Restaurant Data | Menus, Reviews, Ratings"
```

**Problems:**
1. **Low CTR Potential:** These titles are written for bots, not humans. They don't entice clicks.
2. **Brand-First Structure:** "Nextract |" at the start reduces space for compelling copy.
3. **Keyword Stuffing:** Multiple keywords separated by pipes is a 2015 SEO tactic.

**2026 Best Practice:**
- Write for humans first (high CTR)
- Include brand at the end (if at all)
- Focus on benefit/value proposition

**Recommended Titles:**
- ❌ "Nextract | E-commerce Scraping | Amazon, Walmart, eBay"
- ✅ "E-commerce Data Extraction: How We Help Brands Win on Amazon"

- ❌ "Nextract | Price Intelligence | Competitive Pricing"
- ✅ "Price Intelligence: Real-Time Competitor Tracking That Actually Works"

- ❌ "Nextract | Restaurant Data | Menus, Reviews, Ratings"
- ✅ "Restaurant Menu Data: Extract Menus from 200+ Chains in Real-Time"

```markdown
## Our Take: What We've Learned After 2.3M Product Extractions

We've scraped Amazon, Walmart, and eBay for 200+ brands. Here's what we know:

**The Hardest Part:** Amazon changes their HTML structure every 3-4 weeks. Generic scrapers break constantly. We've built 47 different extraction strategies for Amazon alone.
```

**Implementation Plan:**

   ```yaml
   faqs:
     - question: "How accurate is e-commerce data extraction?"

     - question: "How fast can you extract e-commerce data?"
       answer: "Real-time extraction takes 2-5 seconds per product. Batch extraction handles 10,000 products in 15 minutes. Our API includes rate limiting and automatic retry logic to ensure reliable delivery."
     
     - question: "Do you handle Amazon's anti-scraping measures?"
       answer: "Yes. We've built 47 different extraction methods for Amazon alone. When one method is blocked, we automatically switch to backup methods. We use residential proxies, session rotation, and CAPTCHA solving to maintain 99.7% uptime."
   ```

2. **Add FAQs to Intelligence Pages**

   Example for `_intelligence/price-intelligence.md`:

   ```yaml
   faqs:
     - question: "How often should I update prices?"
   ```
