The Challenge

Amazon's algorithm reprices products continuously throughout the day. If you're checking competitor prices manually, you've already lost. Competitors adjust prices frequently, and missing these changes means:

  • → Lost Buy Box wins - Competitors undercut your price while you're sleeping
  • → Destroyed margins - Price too low and lose profit, price too high and lose sales
  • → Missed opportunities - Can't capitalize on competitor price increases
  • → Reactive pricing - Always behind, never ahead of market changes

The Solution

Automated Amazon price monitoring with real-time alerts and Buy Box tracking

Real-Time Price Monitoring

Track competitor prices across hundreds of ASINs. Get instant alerts when prices change. Monitor price history and trends to predict competitor behavior.

Buy Box Tracking

Know exactly who wins the Buy Box and at what price. Track Buy Box ownership changes. Optimize your pricing to win more Buy Box placements.

Price Optimization

Automated repricing recommendations based on competitor data. Balance between winning Buy Box and maintaining margins. A/B test pricing strategies.

Automated Alerts

Get notified instantly when competitors change prices. Set custom price thresholds. Receive daily/weekly pricing reports via email or API.
