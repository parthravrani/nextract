Introduction

Nextract Data Solutions is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the data you provide to us or that we collect during your use of our services.

Data Collection

We collect the following types of information:

  • → Contact Information: When you contact us through our "Get in Touch" forms or via email, we collect your name and email address to respond to your inquiries.
  • → API Usage Logs: To monitor and maintain our API services, we collect request timestamps, IP addresses, API endpoints accessed, tokens used, request counts, response codes, and error logs.
  • → Website Usage Data: Our website, hosted on the Wix platform, uses cookies for functionality. While we don't directly collect this data, Wix's platform does use cookies.

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • → Responding to Inquiries: We use your contact information to respond to your service inquiries.
  • → Service Monitoring and Maintenance: We use API usage logs to monitor service performance, ensure security, and troubleshoot issues.
  • → Website Analytics: We use website analytics from Wix to monitor website performance and SEO effectiveness.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data, including:

  • → Utilizing secure infrastructure provided by AWS and other reputable service providers.
  • → Implementing access control policies to restrict data access to authorized personnel.
  • → Employing industry-standard encryption methods to protect data in transit and at rest.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • → Access: You can request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • → Deletion: You can request the deletion of your personal information.
  • → Contact: To exercise these rights, please contact us via email or our contact forms.

Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection regulations, including:

  • → DMCA: We are DMCA compliant and respect intellectual property rights.
  • → GDPR and CCPA: We follow the regulations for GDPR and CCPA, though we do not hold specific certifications.

Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email hello@nextract.dev or our contact forms.
