Technical Insights

Amazon's anti-scraping measures are constantly evolving. We've processed 2.3M+ ASINs and learned exactly what breaks. Here's what Scrava handles automatically:

  • → CAPTCHAs: Automatically solved using our CAPTCHA-solving infrastructure. No manual intervention needed.
  • → Rotating Proxies: We maintain a pool of residential and datacenter proxies that rotate automatically to avoid IP blocks.
  • → "Dog" Page Errors: When Amazon shows the "Sorry, we just need to make sure you're not a robot" page, Scrava automatically retries with different strategies until it succeeds.
  • → Rate Limiting: Built-in rate limiting prevents triggering Amazon's anti-bot systems while maintaining high throughput.

Result: 99.7% success rate across 2.3M+ ASIN extractions.
